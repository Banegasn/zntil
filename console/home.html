A CLEAR PLACE TO SET THE RULES

Before traffic reaches
your application.

Zntil is a reverse proxy. Public domains point to the gateway; the gateway forwards accepted requests to the upstream service you choose.

ADMIT

Keep traffic bounded.

Per-client and per-proxy request budgets, connection caps, body limits and timeouts put explicit boundaries around incoming work.

INSPECT

Stop common probes.

Reject sensitive-file scans and detect SQL injection or XSS patterns in supported request data. Suspicion scores support temporary bans.

ROUTE

Reach the right origin.

Map domains to private services or verified HTTPS upstreams. Stream responses and support WebSocket upgrades.

CLEAR ABOUT ITS BOUNDARIES

A gateway, with a defined scope.

Zntil is an early implementation, not an audited, comprehensive WAF or a global DDoS absorption network. It does not implement browser challenges or advanced botnet identification.

Keep application authentication, input validation and webhook signatures. CDN cache hits bypass the gateway and are absent from its traffic counts. Hosting and bandwidth costs still apply.
